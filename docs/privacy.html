Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

ConvoGo ("the app", "we") is a translation app for two situations: talking face to face with someone on one shared phone (voice mode), and chatting with someone in another messaging app (text mode). We built it to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no advertising, no tracking.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't build a profile of you.
  • Your conversations are not stored on our servers. The app keeps your current conversation on your phone until you clear it.

What the app uses, and why

Microphone & speech

When you tap a language to speak, the app captures audio from your device's microphone and streams it, over an encrypted connection, to our speech service, which forwards it to a third-party speech-to-text provider (Deepgram) that converts your speech to text in real time. The audio is used only to produce that transcript while you are speaking; we do not record, save, or store it, and it is not tied to your name or account. If the cloud speech service is unavailable, the app falls back to your device's built-in speech recognition (handled by your operating system — Apple on iOS, Google on Android), in which case audio is handled by that platform under its own privacy policy.

Translation

To translate, the app sends the text (your transcribed speech in voice mode, or the message you type or paste in text mode) (and the two language codes, e.g. "en" and "es") over an encrypted connection to our translation service, which forwards it to the Google Cloud Translation API and returns the translation. These requests are not tied to your name, account, or a device identifier. We do not store the text you translate. Google processes the text to perform the translation under its own terms; see Google's privacy policy.

Clipboard (text mode)

In text mode the app reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste, to bring in the message you copied from your chat app. It puts text on your clipboard when you send a message (the translation, ready to paste into your chat) or when you tap a bubble to copy it. The app never reads the clipboard in the background.

Usage limits

To keep the service within its free tier, our server keeps a running count of the total number of characters translated each month. For speech, it also counts the minutes of audio used per installation of the app, so it can apply a fair monthly limit. That count is tied to a random ID the app creates on first use. The ID is not linked to your name, account, phone number or advertising ID, and the counts contain no audio, no text and no personal data.

Your settings and conversations

Your two languages, your settings, and your current conversation in each mode are saved locally on your device, so they are still there when you reopen the app. They are not sent to us or synced anywhere. Tap Clear in the app to delete a conversation; uninstalling the app deletes everything.

What we don't collect

  • No names, emails, or accounts.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, or files.
  • No advertising identifiers or analytics SDKs.
  • No conversation history on our servers — conversations stay on your phone and nothing is synced.

Service providers

Delivering speech-to-text and translation relies on a few infrastructure providers who may process technical data (such as your IP address) only as needed to route and serve requests:

  • Deepgram — cloud speech-to-text; transcribes the audio streamed while you speak.
  • Google Cloud Translation — translates the submitted text.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the lightweight proxy that routes the audio and text.
  • Apple / Google — your device's built-in speech recognition, used only as an offline fallback when the cloud service is unavailable.

Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

Children

ConvoGo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.
